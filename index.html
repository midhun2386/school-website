Our Story

Built on Purpose

For over two decades, Oakwood has been more than a school — it's a community where values, innovation, and academic rigour converge to shape the leaders of tomorrow.

"Education is not just about preparing children for exams — it's about preparing them for life. At Oakwood, every child discovers their own path to greatness."
— Dr. Meera Krishnan, Principal

Founded in 1998 with just 60 students and a vision, Oakwood International School has grown into one of Coimbatore's most respected academic institutions. Our holistic approach to education balances academic excellence with character development, creativity, and community engagement.

🎯

Core Values

Integrity, empathy, and resilience form the bedrock of our educational philosophy, guiding every student and teacher.

🏛️

World-Class Infrastructure

Smart classrooms, science labs, a 25,000-book library, sports complex, and performance arts centre on a lush 8-acre campus.

💡

Teaching Philosophy

Inquiry-based, student-centred learning with small class sizes ensures every child receives individual attention and mentorship.
